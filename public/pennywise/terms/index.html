What the application does

Pennywise reads account information you explicitly authorise, stores it locally in encrypted form, and produces reports from it. It is a reporting tool and nothing else.

Read-only, by design. Pennywise requests account information access only. It cannot initiate, modify, or cancel payments, transfers, or standing orders, and it never requests the permissions that would make that possible.

Your accounts, your authorisation

You may only use Pennywise to access accounts you hold or are lawfully authorised to access. Each account holder completes their bank's own authentication themselves; you cannot authorise someone else's account on their behalf, and you should not attempt to.
