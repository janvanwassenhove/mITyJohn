**A 3D racing game the size of a fish tank.** Ten habitats, each one a tank standing in somebody's living room: glass panes, a cabinet underneath, a light fixture on top. You race around the inside of it.

It runs in the browser, on a keyboard or a phone, with no account and nothing to install.

## Ten tanks, two vehicles

**Five aquariums** — an Amazon blackwater panorama, a planted Mekong cube, a Malawi rock tank, a bow-front Indo-Pacific reef and a cylindrical moon-jelly display. Here you pilot a **mini submarine**, so the course has depth: rise and dive as well as steer.

**Five terrariums** — the Australian outback, the Sonoran desert, a tall Madagascar canopy, a Costa Rican waterfall paludarium and a hexagonal New Caledonian fern display. Here it is a **buggy**, on real terrain: it pitches and banks over the hills, and loses speed and grip in the shallow pools.

Every tank has its own shape, so every circuit has its own silhouette — a meander, an hourglass, a clover, an orbit.

## The locals

The tanks are occupied. Twenty-two species live in them, from cardinal tetras and discus to bearded dragons, geckos, scorpions and chameleons, and none of them agreed to a race.

So they are part of it: collisions with them, the waste they leave behind and a well-timed tongue strike are all hazards of the course. And you carry **food**. Drop it behind you and the local animals steer towards the bait — ideally into the path of whoever is following. Each new lap restocks it.

## A race

Three laps against three computer-driven opponents, through checkpoints you have to take in order. There is a boost that recharges, a minimap of the course, a recover button that puts you back at the last checkpoint for a three-second penalty, and results at the end.

The interface comes in English, French, Dutch and German. Keys can be remapped, and there are three picture-quality settings for slower devices. On a phone a joystick and buttons appear on their own, and after one online visit it installs as an app and plays offline.

## Honest scope

This is a playable prototype, version 0.3, not a finished racer. The opponents follow waypoints, the water is an animated current rather than fluid dynamics, and there is no multiplayer, gamepad support, progression or leaderboard yet.

One more disclaimer, from the game's own notes: the regional themes and real species names are there for variety. The stocking densities of a fantasy race are not animal-care advice.
