**The conference is over. The fight isn't.** Kinepolis Antwerp after the last session: the booths are closed, the auditoriums are empty, and five robots have been left unsupervised. A client-side arcade fighter in the browser — pick a machine, pick an arena, and let the venue join in.

## The roster

Every fighter has its own mass, acceleration, friction, reach, power, jump and recovery, so they do not just look different — they move differently.

- **Voxxy** — the friendly yeeter. A fast all-rounder that throws and slingshots.
- **Droid** — root access granted. Tall, long reach, and able to arm the hazards around it.
- **Biggy** — the absolute unit. Heavy, slow to stop, and very hard to stop once it is not.
- **Richie Mini** — no limbs, no problem. Hops, bonks, throws a tantrum, and plays dead.
- **Microduck** — eight hundred grams of confidence. Tiny rushdown with a beak lock.

Overclock gives any of them five seconds of being more themselves than is strictly safe. Voxxy, Droid and Biggy are the same model-sheet builds as in [Please Do Not Throw Richie](/apps/please-do-not-throw-richie/); Richie is the real Reachy Mini, animated on its actual neck and antenna joints.

## The arena is the sixth fighter

Six slices of the building, each with a hazard of its own: the **exhibition hall** with a booth that flips, the **grand staircase** with a drop either side, the **cinema corridor** when a session lets out, the **auditorium**, where the recliners launch, the **keynote stage** and its lift, and the **robot lab**, where a factory reset switches your specials off.

## How to play

**Arcade** takes you through the roster to an overclocked rival on the keynote stage. **Local versus** puts two players on one keyboard, two controllers, or one of each. **Chaos** is thirty-second rounds with every hazard live. **Training** gives you a dummy and no clock. Keys can be rebound, controllers just work, and on a phone a stick and a fight-stick cluster appear on their own.

It installs from the browser and runs offline after the first visit. No account, no server, no robot required.
