**A 3D hockey game that runs in a browser tab, and an Academy for the real thing.** Three formats — outdoor 11v11, Hockey5s and indoor — played on keyboard, touch or a standard gamepad. Nothing to install, and no account for a match: open [hockeyeleven.com](https://hockeyeleven.com/) and you are on the pitch.

All players and clubs are fictional, and the game has no ties to the FIH, any association or any club.

## Before the menu, a match nobody is playing

The game opens by playing itself, each shot a real match the engine simulates from a seed with no human on either side. Nothing is recorded, so the reel is different every time and adds nothing to the download. Any tap, click or key skips it, and a browser asking for reduced motion never sees it.

## The game

Pick a quick match, or let it surprise you. All **137 FIH nations** are in it, with ratings taken from the official rankings per format, because a nation's standing is not the same outdoors as it is indoors, along with dozens of fictional clubs. Under **My club** you put together your own players, systems and presses.

Penalty corners are played out properly, with routines to call. A rear tackle on someone bearing down on goal is a penalty stroke, repeat offenders sit out on the scoreboard clock, and free hits inside the attacking 23 have to travel five metres before the circle. The formats differ where the real ones do: indoors and in Hockey5s a pass can go off the side boards, and indoors every shot is a push.

Tournaments are played in the formats the real ones use, with a draw from a seed and the other results settled on strength. The **Career** is in beta. New to it? *Learn to play* teaches the controls in short lessons, played in the real simulation. Six languages: Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish and Polish.

## The Academy

*Real hockey to learn, with the engine as board and as film.* Separate from the game, for real players, coaches and trainers, and in beta. It asks for a free account.

- **Technique.** Each skill as a 3D film you can watch from the side, from behind or close up, at full, half or quarter speed, with key points, common faults and the drills that train it.
- **Tactics.** Lessons in phases, played by the game's own players on a match pitch, including what happens when you get it wrong.
- **Drills.** A board in 2D and 3D, step by step, with coaching points, easier and harder versions, the equipment and a print button.
- **Sessions.** A timeline of drills, with examples per age group and a total of what to bring. Print it, or share it to the group chat.
- **Indoor.** Its own techniques, drills and sessions, because half of what works outside is not allowed inside.

At the end of a quick match the game looks at your own numbers, picks one thing, and offers the skill that helps.

## Online

Play another human from your browser, with an account. Both sides run the whole simulation in lockstep, and only your inputs travel.

## Play it

It lives at **[hockeyeleven.com](https://hockeyeleven.com/)**, and it installs as an offline app if you ask it to.
